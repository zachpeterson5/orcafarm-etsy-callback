Return to Omni Farm

If you just approved Etsy access, copy the complete address from your browser's address bar.

  1. Switch back to Omni Farm and open Orders.
  2. Paste that address into “Full callback URL after approval”.
  3. Select “Finish connection”. Omni Farm will verify the response.

If you declined access or arrived here directly, return to Omni Farm to start a new authorization.

The address may contain a temporary authorization code. Paste it only into Omni Farm, not into a chat or message.
