Return to OrcaFarm

If you just approved Etsy access, copy the complete address from your browser's address bar.

  1. Switch back to OrcaFarm and open Orders.
  2. Paste that address into “Full callback URL after approval”.
  3. Select “Finish connection”. OrcaFarm will verify the response.

If you declined access or arrived here directly, return to OrcaFarm to start a new authorization.

The address may contain a temporary authorization code. Paste it only into OrcaFarm, not into a chat or message.
